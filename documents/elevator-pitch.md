Generate a concise elevator pitch document from the concept documents. This is a **one-page** document that communicates the product's value proposition clearly and quickly.

## Structure

1. **Headline** — One sentence that captures the product's essence
2. **Problem** — What pain point does this solve? (2-3 sentences)
3. **Solution** — What does the product do? (2-3 sentences)
4. **Key Benefits** — 3-5 bullet points, outcome-focused (not feature-focused)
5. **Target Audience** — Who is this for?
6. **Differentiator** — What makes this different from alternatives?
7. **Call to Action** — Next step (demo, trial, contact)

## Tone & Style

- Persuasive but factual
- No jargon — understandable by non-technical stakeholders
- Every sentence must earn its place — ruthlessly concise
- Focus on outcomes and value, not technology
- Maximum one page when rendered as PDF
